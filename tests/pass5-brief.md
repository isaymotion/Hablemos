# Hablemos pass 5 — shared drafting brief (practice cases)

Hablemos is a Spanish/English web app for English-speaking clinicians interviewing Spanish-speaking psychiatry patients in the US. It is the sister of a Tagalog app (Usap Tayo) and shares its JavaScript engine, so **data shapes and field names are fixed**; the content is new Spanish for the US/Latino context. You are writing ONE data file in `/home/claude/hablemos/js/data/` (told in your task). Do not touch other files.

## Hard rules
- Plain JavaScript data files, same shape as the header comment in the shell file you are replacing. Keep the header comment, update it to say **"STATUS: DRAFT FOR REVIEW by a native Spanish-speaking clinician"** (replace "EMPTY SHELL" wording). Field names `tl`/`f`/`c` stay (engine): `tl` = Spanish text. Clinician lines (case `choices[].tl`) use **usted** unless the patient is a child or the register obviously calls for tú.
- Spanish: US/Mexican baseline, natural spoken register for patients (patients may use regional/colloquial words: ándale, bien, la cruda, malilla, las azules/M30, cristal, mota, perico, la migra, coraje, nervios, ataque de nervios, susto, mal de ojo, brujería/"un trabajo", curandero, botánica, el Señor/Dios, "me van a deportar"). Gender-mark adjectives for the speaker (decaída for a woman). Use ¿…? ¡…! and accents correctly. English translations are natural US clinical English.
- **Forbidden anywhere** (grep before finishing): `po`, `kayo`, `opo`, `ninyo`, `Dok`, `Tagalog`, `Filipino`, `Mariveles`, `NCMH`, `1553`, `barangay`, `shabu`, `betel`, `nganga`, `peso`, `MMH-`, `tanod`, `Aling`, `Mang`, `Lola`. Crisis line is **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, "oprima 2" for Spanish). Hospital is unnamed ("the hospital", "la clínica").
- Fictional patients only: first names (or Don/Doña + name), age, occupation. No surnames, record numbers, real staff names, dates of birth. The clinician is "doctor/doctora" (no name).
- Clinical content: DSM-5-TR, standard US inpatient psychiatry practice; medication examples with generic names and realistic adult doses, marked as teaching examples. Don't reproduce copyrighted instruments (no MMSE wording, no C-SSRS text); paraphrase.
- No stigmatizing terms as correct answers (loco, drogadicto); they may appear as score-0 choices with feedback explaining why.
- Writing style of English feedback/notes: plain, active, no em-dash asides, short sentences. Feedback (`fb`, `why`) teaches the interviewing skill, like Usap Tayo's (see examples).
- When done: `cd /home/claude/hablemos && node /home/claude/hablemos-dev/tests/data_check.js .` must print `DATA OK` with no ERROR line mentioning your file (WARN lines are fine). Also `node -e "require('vm')..."` is not needed; data_check loads everything. Fix every ERROR.
- Report back: counts, the 5–10 Spanish lines you are least sure of (for the native-speaker reviewer), and anything you had to interpret.
