## Shared data shapes of this pass

**`window.DICT_EX`** (files `js/data/examples-<n>.js`): `window.DICT_EX = (window.DICT_EX || []).concat([ [key, es, en, lv, hit?], … ]);`
- `key` = `'<headword>|<English>'`: the first two fields of ONE line of `js/data/dict-*.js`, copied EXACTLY (write a script that reads the real lines; never type keys by hand).
- `es`: one natural Spanish sentence of 4 to 12 words that uses the word in the sense of that line. `en`: its English. `lv`: `'A1' | 'A2' | 'B1' | 'B2'` = the level of the sentence (its grammar and the word together; syllabus in pass8-brief).
- `hit`: the exact word (or adjacent words) of `es` that is the headword in this sentence. Leave it out when the headword stands in the sentence exactly as written (case aside). Required when the form differs: a conjugated verb (`dormir` → `"duermo"`), a plural or feminine (`cansado` → `"cansada"`), a reflexive (`sentirse` → `"me siento"`). `hit` must occur in `es` exactly once as whole words. A headword with an article or several words (`el dolor de cabeza`) must appear whole or have a `hit`.
- One sentence per key. About half could be said on a psychiatry ward, clinic or ED (patient, relative, nurse, doctor using usted), half everyday. No sentence twice. The blanked sentence must point to this word and not to a near-synonym in the same file where you can manage it (it becomes a fill-in question with other dictionary words as wrong options).
- The audio pack records every `es`.

**`window.LISTEN_Q`**: `window.LISTEN_Q = Object.assign(window.LISTEN_Q || {}, { '<dialogue or reading id>': [[question, [4 options], answerIndex, why], …] });` Questions, options and `why` in English. 3 questions per dialogue, 3 or 4 per reading. The learner HEARS the whole text first and cannot see it, so ask about what is said (who, what, when, how long, what the doctor asks them to do), never about spelling. One clearly right option; spread the right index over 0 to 3 across the file. Do not repeat a reading's own `q` questions.
