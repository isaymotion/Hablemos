# Hablemos pass 6 — shared drafting brief (learn track)

Hablemos is a Spanish/English web app for English-speaking clinicians (US psychiatry residents and nurses) interviewing Spanish-speaking patients. Pass 6 fills the **learn track**: lessons, clinical dictionary, readings, dialogues and a placement test. The engine is shared with a Tagalog app, so **data shapes are fixed** (documented in the header comment of each shell file in `/home/claude/hablemos/js/data/`). You write ONE or TWO files named in your task. Do not touch other files.

## Hard rules
- Audience: English speakers at A1–B1 Spanish who need **clinical** Spanish fast. Every lesson, reading and dialogue should earn its place on a psychiatry ward or clinic; general grammar is taught through clinical examples (sentir, dormir, doler, tener miedo, tomar la medicina).
- Spanish: US/Mexican baseline, natural register; mention common variants where they matter (platicar/hablar, ahorita, coraje, enojado/enfadado, carro/coche, computadora). Patients speak naturally; clinician lines use **usted** unless speaking to a child/teen (tú). Accents and ¿¡ always correct. English is plain US clinical English.
- **Forbidden anywhere** (grep before finishing): `po`, `kayo`, `opo`, `ninyo`, `Dok`, `Tagalog`, `Filipino`, `Mariveles`, `NCMH`, `1553`, `barangay`, `shabu`, `betel`, `nganga`, `MMH-`, `Aling`, `Mang`, `Lola`, `Kuya`, `Ate`, `RA 11036`. Crisis line is **988** ("oprima 2" for Spanish). No real hospital, staff or patient names; fictional first names only.
- Writing style of English text: plain, active, short sentences, no em-dash asides. HTML in lesson bodies: `<p>`, `<h3>`, `<ul><li>`, `<table><tr><th><td>`, `<div class="ex"><b>Spanish</b><span>English</span></div>` for example sentences (each `.ex` is recorded as audio and gets a speaker button; keep the `<b>` text plain Spanish with no HTML inside), `<div class="note">…</div>` for clinical tips. Escape `&` as `&amp;` and avoid `<`/`>` in text.
- Mark drafts: lessons `draft: true`; file headers already say DRAFT FOR REVIEW.
- Do not reproduce copyrighted material (no song lyrics, no published instrument text, no textbook passages). Readings, dialogues and lessons are original.
- Validate: `cd /home/claude/hablemos && node /home/claude/hablemos-dev/tests/data_check.js .` must print `DATA OK` with no ERROR mentioning your file (WARN lines are fine). Also load your file alone: `node -e "const vm=require('vm');const c=vm.createContext({});c.window=c;vm.runInContext(require('fs').readFileSync('js/data/<file>','utf8'),c);console.log(Object.keys(c))"`.
- Report back: counts, the 8–10 Spanish lines you are least sure of (for the native-speaker reviewer), and anything you interpreted.

## Where things live
- Dictionary categories (exact keys, from `App.CATS` in `js/core.js`): basics, grammar, pronouns, questions, numbers, time, people, family, body, food, home, money, places, adjectives, modals, verbs, emotions, beliefs, idioms, health, mind, risk, substance, stigma, clinical, falsefriends. Clinical decks are built from: health, mind, risk, substance, clinical, stigma, beliefs, emotions, idioms, falsefriends.
- Dictionary line format: `spanish | english | pos | category | root | note`. pos ∈ nm nf n v adj adv pron part conj prep interj num phr (use nm/nf for nouns). `root` = lemma: infinitive for verbs/verb forms, singular masculine for adjectives, headword for idioms. `note` = short usage/clinical note (regional variant, false friend warning, register). Multi-word entries are fine (`ataque de nervios`). One sense per line; a second sense is a second line. Lines starting with `#` are comments. The parser dedupes identical spanish+english pairs.
- Lessons with `psych: true` appear under "Related lessons" in the psychiatry hub. The lesson `id` must be a slug; `interpreting` is a required id (the handoff and cross-app links use it).
- The existing phrase sections (`js/data/psych-phrases.js`) and replies (`psych-answers.js`) show the Spanish the app already uses; reuse that wording where lessons quote interview questions.
- Reference: the Tagalog app's equivalents are dumped (English structure only) in `/tmp/p6/ut-learn.md` so you can see the granularity and the quiz style. Do not translate them; write Spanish-specific content.
