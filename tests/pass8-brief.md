Its Japanese sister app **Hanashimashō** has far more grammar lessons and drills. Pass 8 ports those drills and widens the lesson syllabus. ## Content rules
- Audience: English speakers, A1–B2 Spanish, who need clinical Spanish. General grammar is taught through examples that could be heard on a psychiatry ward, clinic or ED (sentir, dormir, doler, tener miedo, tomar la medicina, oír voces), mixed with everyday ones. About half clinical, half everyday.
- Spanish: US/Mexican baseline, natural register. Clinician lines use **usted** unless speaking to a child or teen. Accents, ñ, ¿ ¡ always correct. Note regional variants only where they matter. English: plain US clinical English.
- Every sentence shown as correct must be correct and natural: it is spoken by a recorded voice. Wrong options must be clearly wrong for the reason the item teaches, never a second acceptable answer (check ser/estar, por/para, preterite/imperfect, subjunctive items twice for this).
- Safety: no method details for suicide or self-harm, no doses that could guide an overdose, no product-name misuse instructions. Risk questions are asked the way the app already asks them (`js/data/psych-phrases.js`, section `suicide`). Never produce command, "let's" or encouraging forms of suicidarse, matarse, morir(se), lastimarse, cortarse, hacerse daño.
- No real hospital, staff or patient names; fictional first names only (the app's roster: Mateo, Lupe, Brayan, Ramiro, Yesenia, Jorge, Don Chuy, Doña Rosa, Marisol, Lety; clinician "la doctora Navarro"). Crisis line is 988 (oprima 2). No stigmatizing words as model language (loco, adicto, drogadicto) except when teaching that they are stigmatizing.
- Forbidden strings anywhere (grep before finishing): `po`, `kayo`, `opo`, `ninyo`, `Dok`, `Tagalog`, `Filipino`, `Mariveles`, `NCMH`, `1553`, `barangay`, `shabu`, `betel`, `MMH-`, `Aling`, `Mang`, and anything Japanese (`《`, kana, kanji, `JLPT`, `N5`…`N2`, `romaji`, `furigana`, `JA.`).
- Do not reproduce copyrighted material (no textbook sentences, no published instrument text, no song lyrics).
- UI copy and explanations: plain, active, short sentences, no em-dash asides. One `h1` at the top of every view (the shell adds the breadcrumb and pin button).
- Every data file header says `DRAFT FOR REVIEW by a native Spanish-speaking clinician`, documents the shape, and says ids are permanent (append only after release). JS strings: prefer backticks or escaped quotes; a stray `"` inside a double-quoted string breaks the whole app. After writing, load each file alone: `node -e "const vm=require('vm');const c=vm.createContext({});c.window=c;vm.runInContext(require('fs').readFileSync('<file>','utf8'),c);console.log(Object.keys(c))"`.

## Lesson syllabus (ids are fixed; drills reference them in `l`)
Existing (file `js/data/lessons.js`): A1 sounds, ser-estar, gender-agreement, tu-usted, questions, negation, numbers-dates · A2 present-tense, reflexive-feelings, gustar-doler, past-timelines, commands-instructions, body-symptoms, medicines-dosing · B1 subjunctive-basics, regional-variety, spanglish, softeners, values, faith-folk, idioms-distress, false-friends, interpreting.

New in 0.7.0 (level, unit, id: scope):
- **A1** (`lessons-2.js`)
  - Foundations: `articles-contractions` (el/la/los/las, un/una, al, del; el agua) · `subject-pronouns` (dropping them, usted/ustedes, when to keep them) · `hay-estar` (hay vs está/están, hay que is NOT here) · `possessives` (mi, tu, su and the ambiguity of su; de usted, de él) · `demonstratives` (este/ese/aquel, esto/eso, aquí/ahí/allí) · `basic-prepositions` (a, de, en, con, sin, personal a) · `muy-mucho` (muy, mucho/a/os/as, poco, bastante, demasiado)
  - First verbs: `tener-expressions` (tener hambre, sueño, miedo, frío, años, ganas de, dolor de) · `ir-a-future` (ir a + infinitive; voy a, vamos a) · `stem-changing` (e→ie, o→ue, e→i in the present: querer, poder, dormir, sentir, pedir, pensar, recordar) · `irregular-yo` (tengo, hago, pongo, salgo, digo, oigo, veo, sé, conozco, doy, estoy, voy) · `querer-poder-necesitar` (verb + infinitive: quiero, puedo, necesito, debo, prefiero)

- **A2** (`lessons-3.js`)

Lesson shape (same as `js/data/lessons.js`, read two lessons there first): `{ id, level, unit, title, summary, body: HTML, quiz: [[question, [3 or 4 options], correctIndex, explanation], … 6–8 (12 for reviews)], draft: true, after?: '<lesson id>', review?: true }`. Body HTML: `<p>`, `<h3>`, `<ul><li>`, `<table><tr><th><td>`, `<div class="ex"><b>Spanish sentence</b><span>English</span></div>` (8–12 per lesson; the `<b>` text is plain Spanish with no HTML inside; it is recorded), `<div class="note">…</div>` for clinical tips, and one "Common mistakes" `<h3>` section with three wrong → right pairs written as `<p><s>wrong Spanish</s> → <b>right Spanish</b>. Why, in one sentence.</p>`. Escape `&` as `&amp;`. New files append: `window.LESSONS = (window.LESSONS || []).concat([ … ]);`.
